---
title: Agency
---
I am firmly of the belief that if you want to achieve something where your output is the determining factor in whether you achieve it or not, then you truly have the outcome (mostly) in your hand. While this may sound trivial, it is *agency* which will ultimately help you achieve a certain outcome. Did you reach out to and build the appropriate relationship(s) with the right people? Did you do the appropriate background reading for the task? Did you execute the right subtasks of the original task? There are many more similar questions that you could ask, and it is ultimately upon you to make sure the answer is yes to most (if not all) of them.
