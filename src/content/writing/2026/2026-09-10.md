---
title: Greedy Algorithms
---
I think a lot of daily life can be approached via a greedy algorithm. If you are trying to solve a problem, say, you got a flat tire on your car, doing the *next best thing* will often help. How one defines what is the current "best" action to take is very dependent on the specific scenario you are in, but for everyday tasks you generally won't have that many options and your brain may already assign proabilities and/or rewards for the current action space. I want to think of a concrete example of using this form of thinking, and will probably write about it in a later post.
