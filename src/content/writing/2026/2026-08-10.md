---
title: Context Switching
---
I usually get the most work done on a particular task when I sit down for an extended period of time (usually a few hours, or the amount of time required to complete the task if shorter). The action of working on multiple things in parallel and moving between these often &mdash; context switching &mdash; significantly decreases my productivity and is something I am actively trying to reduce. Even if each of these parallel tasks has my full focus during their duration, there seems to be a period of time that it takes for my brain to adjust to the new task before I enter into flow state. Context switching completely eliminates this adjustment period until I start the next task or take a break.
