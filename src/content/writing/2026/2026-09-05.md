title: Traditions
---
I always love this time of the year, with the fall starting (my favorite season), reconnecting with people I haven't seen in a few months, etc. But the best part is by far the U.S. Open, which, if you don't know, is one of the four biggest tennis tournaments in the world held New York City every year. I've been going with my family since I was probably six or seven years old and it's always an amazing time. The crowd, energy of NYC, and weather makes for a great day. Tennis is an interesting sport, especially singles tennis. Being isolated on the court against your opponent, having to problem-solve individually, and manage the physical aspect of the game in conjunction with the psycological aspect add several interesting dimensions to the game.
