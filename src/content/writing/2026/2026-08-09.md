---
title: Conviction
---
In June, I listened to a podcast by Redpoint Ventures featuring Sebastian Mallaby, the author of several popular biographies on successful industry leaders and academics. In it, he describes Demis Hassabis' conversation with Mark Zuckerberg over lunch on what fields Meta was interested in pursuing in the future. Hassabis probed Zuckerberg on several notable fields, including AI, virtual reality, social media, etc. and Zuckerberg showed equal interest in each of these fields. It was supposedly at this moment when Hassabis knew not to join (and maybe even bet on) Meta, as it did not have the same conviction on the transformative nature of AI as he and presumably the rest of Google DeepMind did.

I think it is very important to have this mentality to achieve ultra success in any field, from sports to software to music to finance to law to medicine. While this sounds simple, it is often easy to rely on a Plan B if your original plan doesn't work, which may imply you simply did not have enough conviction in that original plan.
