---
title: Gratitude
---
Recently, I've been feeling more grateful for the things in live that I *do* have and for what I have *already* achieved. It seems simple to constantly be forward-looking and think about the next milestone in a specific part of my life. While this is an important mentality to have, I think it is important to be grateful for what I already have. This grounds me, and reminds me that I am already where (or ahead of where) my past self would have imagined some amount of years/months/days ago.
