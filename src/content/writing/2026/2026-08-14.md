---
title: Reading
---
It amazes me how much knowledge one can obtain if they learn to read properly and retain the information they read. A book, blog post, even Twitter/X thread is essentially a distillation of an author's views (usually) over some long-horizon. This distillation yields a tremendous amount of information, of which even 50% is retained (ideally more, this can be improved over time), gives a vast amount of knowledge to the reader. As such, I have started to read more consistently, making it a part of my day in some form (whether a book, newsletter, academic paper, article, etc.).
